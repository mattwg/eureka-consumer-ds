# Consumer DS — Common Questions

## Bucket: Weekly / Monthly / MTD metric review
- "What's our [KPI] for [time period] and how does it look by [dimension] YoY?"
- "How is the [dimension] mix shift changed over [time period]?"
- "How are we tracking on cash, NPLs and NRLs this month vs the same period last year — and which region's driving the gap?"
- "How's retention looking right now — first renewal (M1→M2) versus repeat renewals (M2+)?"
- "Where are we on total payers month-to-date, and how does that trend against last month and last year?"

## Bucket: Ad-hoc segment lookups
- "How's the C+ monthly 40%-off-3-months promo doing? Can I see a 4-week trend across our major product sub-types — promo vs non-promo?"
- "What does retention look like from M1 all the way to M12 for 40%-discount subs vs upsell vs full-price?"

## Bucket: Retention rates
- "What are M1, M2 and M2+ retention for [C Plus monthly], and where's the biggest drop-off?"
- "How does retention compare for monthly vs annual plans, splitting first renewal from repeat (1+) renewals?"
- "What is the completion rate drop-off for terminal subs and how has it trended over time?"
- "Show the full 12-month retention curve for the [Jan 2026] C Plus monthly cohort, and compare it year-over-year."
- "Can we decompose YoY retention rate change by [dimension] — how much is each [dimension] contributing to the change?"

## Bucket: Acquisition → conversion
- "How are total registrations trending by geo/device, and what % convert to paid within 14 days?"
- "Which marketing channel drives the most NPLs — and how well do those subscribers retain?"
- "What is the conversion rate from new visits to NPLs and how is it trending channel-wise?"

## Bucket: Movement explanations (exact questions from stakeholders)
- "M2 retention fell off a cliff around March 17, 2026 — and M1→M2 is sliding too. What's driving it, and which segment is worst hit?"
- "We are seeing a surge in E2C traffic since around March 11, 2026, and Direct dipped at the same time — is that real, or are we just re-attributing Direct visits to E2C?"
- "Registrations are down on the home page over the last 3 weeks — can we figure out what's behind it?"

---

**KPIs:** Cash, NPL, Retention, Total Payers, New Visits, NRLs
**Dimensions:** Geo, Channel, Product Sub Type
